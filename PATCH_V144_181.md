# Spreelo v144.181 — Editorial Quality + Post Type Credit Controls

Built on **v144.180 HARDENED**.

## What changed

### Customer-facing content types
The five established product formats are intentionally left on their existing generation contracts:
- Product post
- Product ad
- Animated product Reel
- AI product video
- Product carousel

The editorial set is simplified to six active formats:
- Problem & solution
- Tips & knowledge
- Question & answer
- Guide & decision help
- Service in focus
- Engagement & humour

The old overlapping editorial cards remain readable for historical plans but are disabled for new selection and mapped to the new set:
- Common mistakes -> Tips & knowledge
- Myth vs fact -> Tips & knowledge
- Seasonal post -> Tips & knowledge / contextual seasonality
- Checklist -> Guide & decision help
- Mini guide -> Guide & decision help
- Previous retired editorial ids continue to map safely for history/rescue compatibility

Seasonality is now optional context for editorial planning rather than a standalone post type. Seasonal campaign signals do not force the Engagement & humour format; the planner can still choose the best-fitting editorial type.

### Editorial quality engine
A shared editorial quality contract now requires specific, useful, brand-relevant ideas and rejects generic filler. It also prevents unsupported company-specific claims.

- **Problem & solution** starts from a real audience problem and prefers a verified product/service only when it genuinely solves it. It can fall back to a useful non-product solution instead of inventing a sales connection.
- **Tips & knowledge** can intelligently use angles such as practical tip, common mistake, myth/fact, did-you-know or things-to-consider without exposing those as separate post types.
- **Question & answer** prefers verified website/brand evidence and must not invent company policies, prices, delivery terms, guarantees or service inclusions.
- **Guide & decision help** can choose checklist, step-by-step, selection guide or decision-help structure based on the idea.
- **Service in focus** is only exposed when a concrete service has been independently verified. Mixed product + service businesses are supported.
- **Engagement & humour** is optimized for natural reactions/comments/shares without promising virality or forcing humour.

### Engagement & humour media selector
Manual creation defaults to **AI video (10 sec)** and allows the customer to switch before generation to:
- AI video · 10 sec
- AI image
- Product image (only when a verified product image is available)

The displayed/reserved credit price follows the selected media variant. Automatic/adaptive planning uses a static image path by default so it cannot silently incur the expensive video price without an explicit customer choice.

### Rescue
Rescue receives the same editorial quality contract and preserves the intended editorial post type. Problem & solution can still use verified product-first Rescue where appropriate, while safe fallback remains available if a product cannot be verified.

### Verified service evidence
Brand analysis stores service availability independently of product/catalog mode:
- `website_service_mode_available`
- `website_service_mode_checked_at`
- `website_service_mode_reason`
- `website_service_source_url`

This prevents `Service in focus` from appearing merely because a brand looks like a service business.

### Admin — content types & credit pricing
Admin `Content & Credits` now supports central database-driven pricing for all post types and selectable variant prices for Engagement & humour.

For Engagement & humour Admin can independently set:
- AI video credit price
- AI image credit price
- Product image credit price

The selected price is read from the same central content-format source used by customer UI/runtime. Existing Admin-edited base prices are preserved by the migration where possible.

### i18n
English remains the canonical source language. New/renamed customer-visible and Admin-visible strings use i18n keys in the existing translation system. The translation cache version is bumped so translated locale packages can be regenerated/cached through the existing DB-backed translation flow.

## Database migration
Run before deploying the application:

`supabase/v144_181_editorial_quality_post_types_and_credit_controls.sql`

The migration is additive/idempotent and keeps historical content-format rows so existing plans remain readable.
