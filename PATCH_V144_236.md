# Spreelo v144.236 — Shopify brand-level onboarding

## Why
The Shopify App Store onboarding previously treated "first-time" as an account with zero brand workspaces. That is too broad for multi-brand Spreelo accounts and makes testing unsafe when one account owns many brands.

## Change
- Shopify onboarding now decides whether to show the existing analysis summary from the selected Shopify brand's `analysis_required` state, not from the account-wide brand count.
- A newly created or not-yet-analyzed Shopify brand goes through the normal AI analysis, the shared `/onboarding/ready` summary, then Social channels.
- An already matched and already analyzed brand returns to Grow Brain without repeating onboarding.
- No database migration is required.

This keeps onboarding brand/store specific and avoids deleting unrelated workspaces just to test App Store onboarding.
