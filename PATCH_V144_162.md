# Spreelo v144.162 — Product Research Cost Guard

Baseline: **v144.161**

## Why this patch exists

A Köttfabriken single-image product post followed an unnecessarily expensive path even though Spreelo already had usable local product evidence. The run continued into GPT web research, consumed more than 42k research tokens, and kept collecting products until the historical maximum pool was reached.

This patch keeps web research as an important resilience layer for blocked or difficult stores, but makes it a **fallback rather than a routine second opinion**.

## Changes

### 1. Ranked local product can finish the post before paid web research

For whole-site single-product posts, Spreelo now first gives Store Map, Product Engine, the ranked verified catalog and store search a chance to finish the product lock.

The local catalog candidate is not simply the first product found. It has already passed the existing ranking, recent-use/diversity and campaign/product-fit logic. Spreelo then tries to lock that exact selected product deterministically. If that succeeds, the post continues immediately without paid domain web research.

### 2. Web research remains available when it is actually needed

The fallback is retained for cases such as:

- 403 / anti-bot / security-blocked product sources
- 429 / rate-limited product sources
- no verified local product
- local verified product cannot be safely locked
- local/store-search candidates are exhausted or unusable

Protected sources receive one bounded indexed web-research rescue pass before Admin Rescue. This intentionally supersedes the older v144.107 fail-fast policy that stopped protected sources before indexed research.

There is **no hard money cutoff** that aborts a valid post merely because it has become expensive.

### 3. Single-product web research stops much earlier

A single-product post still gets a small shortlist for resilience and editorial choice:

- target: **2 verified products**
- initial research request: up to **3 strong candidates**
- stop as soon as the requested verified-product target is reached

The previous generic behavior could continue toward the historical maximum of 8 verified products even when only one final product was needed.

### 4. Bad internal search queries are filtered

Product Engine now rejects narrow internal workflow phrases such as:

- `one verified with`
- `one verified`
- `verified with usable`
- `verified with`

Real product searches such as `entrecote`, `notkott`, `grillkott`, `farskt kott` and legitimate phrases containing words such as `certified` remain valid.

Generated and persisted product-intent search-query metadata now uses the same sanitizer before retailer search.

### 5. Clear product-discovery logging

Success and fallback paths now log fields such as:

- `productDiscoveryPath`
- `fallbackReason`
- `paidWebResearchUsed`

Typical paths include:

- `product_engine_store_map`
- `product_engine_ranked_catalog`
- `product_engine_store_search`
- `web_research_fallback`

This makes future cost spikes much easier to diagnose from one run log.

## Failure-safety / regression scope

- Existing exact product/page identity safety remains in place.
- Web research was **not removed**.
- 403 and 429 rescue paths are explicitly retained.
- A local product still has to be acceptable and safely locked before the paid fallback is skipped.
- Social Channels is untouched and remains byte-for-byte identical to the verified v144.159–v144.161 baseline.
- v144.159 language/default changes, v144.160 prompt-language/product-title safety, and v144.161 automation prerender hotfix remain intact.

## Database

No SQL or database migration is required.
