# Spreelo v144.173 — Five-product rotation safety

Base: **v144.171 Campaign Rescue Validation**. v144.172 is deliberately not included.

## Purpose
Keep the existing carousel creative/rendering pipeline unchanged while making product rotation safer: an ordinary five-product carousel must not fail only because otherwise valid verified products have been used before.

## Changes
- Ordinary carousels still prefer unused verified products first.
- If fewer than five unused products are available, the selector may fill the remaining slots from previously used verified products.
- Reused products are ordered conservatively: fewer uses first, then oldest `last_used_at` first.
- Ordinary `whole_website` carousels continue to use the verified brand-wide catalog pool.
- Ordinary `product_category` / `focus_page` carousels may now early-exit from their already verified **source-scoped** catalog pool. They do not pull unrelated brand-wide products into a focused category.
- Focused calendar-campaign carousels try fresh theme-safe products first, then may reuse only products that still pass the existing campaign safety filter.
- The existing general calendar-campaign relevance pipeline, campaign fit scoring, delivery ladder and senior final review remain intact.

## Explicitly unchanged
- Exactly five carousel slides/products.
- GPT-5.6 Sol carousel creative planning.
- GPT-Image carousel prompts/rendering.
- Product identity review.
- Campaign-theme selection and senior review.
- Rescue validation from v144.171.
- No v144.172 compositor/fallback-renderer changes are included.
