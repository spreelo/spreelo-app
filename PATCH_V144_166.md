# Spreelo v144.166 — Product Discovery & Category Diversity

## Scope
This patch builds on v144.165 and only changes product discovery / product-image safety.
No database migration or SQL is required.

## Changes
- Known Store Map category/brand/campaign URLs are treated as navigation shelves rather than product candidates. Focused discovery follows those shelf links to real product pages instead of repeatedly trying to verify listing pages as products.
- Normal recurring single-product plans inspect a broader set of Store Map shelves and receive a mild recent-category diversity score. Quality and product relevance still win; this is not a hard category quota.
- Real calendar campaigns remain campaign-first. `queue_source = "campaign"` keeps the existing campaign shelf strategy and does not receive the generic category-rotation score.
- Retailer search-query sanitation now rejects leaked workflow fragments such as `verified`, `with`, and `with usable` while preserving genuine product searches.
- A single verification cache is reused through the local single-product discovery occurrence so already-classified URLs are not needlessly re-verified by later local fallbacks.
- Product-image safety adds a deterministic multi-signal variant guard. A suspicious asset is rejected before semantic vision when at least two independent explicit dimensions disagree between the locked product title and image URL (for example 5-pack + 290 g versus 6-pack + 350 g). One differing filename number alone is not enough to reject.
- Store Map diagnostics now expose shelf selection breadth, known listing count and distinct product shelf count; focused discovery logs listing traversal/skips.

## Preserved behavior
- GPT-5.6 Sol locked/proofread image-headline flow from v144.165 is unchanged.
- Paid web research remains available as the bounded fallback when local verified discovery genuinely cannot complete.
- Calendar-campaign relevance remains ahead of generic category diversity.
- Social Channels is unchanged.
