# Spreelo v144.269 — Billing scheduled-change flexibility

## Fixes
- A scheduled downgrade no longer freezes every plan action.
- The customer can cancel the pending downgrade by choosing/keeping the current plan.
- The customer can switch the current plan from monthly to yearly while a downgrade is pending.
- The customer can immediately upgrade while a downgrade is pending; the old Stripe schedule is released first.
- Choosing a different future downgrade replaces the previous schedule instead of returning a blocking 409.
- Pending-plan fields and the old audit row are cleared when the Stripe schedule is released.

## Credits
- Existing paid-plan upgrade behavior is intentionally preserved: same-interval upgrades grant only the prorated difference between plan allowances for the remaining billing period. Old plan credits are not stacked on top of the new plan allowance.
- Purchased extra credits remain separate and are not reset by these changes.

No SQL migration is required for v144.269.
