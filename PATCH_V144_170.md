# Spreelo v144.170 — Carousel-specific product identity review

- Kling/video identity review is unchanged.
- Failed carousel reviews now log the individual identity gates for diagnosis.
- v144.169 ordinary-carousel verified-catalog selection and calendar-campaign theme/product matching remain unchanged.
