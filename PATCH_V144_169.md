# Spreelo v144.169 — ordinary carousel shared product pool

## What changed

- Ordinary whole-site product carousels now load the brand-wide verified product catalog before fresh website discovery.
- If five fresh verified products are already available, the carousel selects them immediately and skips the expensive Store Map rediscovery pass.
- This aligns ordinary carousel product sourcing with the verified product pool that already works for normal product posts.
- Product rotation and duplicate-image protection remain active.

## Calendar campaigns are intentionally unchanged

Calendar-campaign carousels (`queue_source = campaign`) do **not** use the new ordinary catalog shortcut. They retain the existing campaign-first pipeline, including:

- saved `product_match_terms`
- saved `product_search_queries`
- campaign theme contract
- campaign-safe candidate filtering
- AI campaign-fit scoring
- senior final review / curation of the five theme-fitting products

This prevents a generic product pool from weakening campaign relevance.

## Database

No SQL migration is required for v144.169.
