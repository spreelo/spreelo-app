## Behavior

- Kling and pixel-preserving animations inspect actual sampled compositions and the returned closing-background image in one bounded vision request (25-second timeout, no API retry). The detector supplies product/print, face and hand rectangles per image. A deterministic layout search protects their union across frames, keeps the corner-logo area free, and prioritizes readable scale. These are general product rules, not category-specific scripts or fixed headline wording.
- Scene lettering and closing lettering receive separate contrast decisions against their actual backgrounds. The approved exact glyphs, word order and typographic proportions remain; mixed backgrounds can get a soft local gradient and subtle glyph shadow. No extra Kling generation is submitted for layout or contrast.
- The actual returned closing background is checked rather than assuming its prompt was followed. A generated product photograph on a closing background is replaced with a frame of the already verified source instead of trusting a new product rendition. Unknown/crowded closing compositions use a quiet branded background without another paid image request. If no verified scene-safe text zone exists, the scene stays unobstructed and the approved message appears on the closing card; this is explicit metadata passed through both reviews, not an admin rejection.
- New closing cards contain no embedded sender/logo. The selected authentic logo remains at the top-left corner on a single independent layer through the entire commercial. No replacement of the test account's Spreelo logo, no account/configuration changes, and no change to the Shotstack stage watermark. The closing transition for new assets is shortened to 0.12 seconds.
- Ready Kling posts persist `finalization_control.state=completed`, clear future retry/poll controls, and preserve paid source/render IDs and audit history. A downstream delivery failure still leaves the finished video ready.
- A worker-level database access failure gets a warning with worker scope, the actual cause and an instruction to check subsequent cron invocations. It does not claim an individual video failed, print `Post: undefined`, or give a text-design recovery instruction. Concrete post-specific failures retain critical incident handling and existing durable email deduplication.
- Health history records the first observed failed check, not only two consecutive failures. Actual database failures in the Kling batch and stale-occurrence lookup also feed this history when the database permits persistence. Existing matching runtime incidents appear as point observations, without inventing an outage duration or claiming a provider-wide outage.
- Availability is reported to three decimals, over sampled intervals only, with latest check time and 30-day coverage. A new/missing ledger shows no measured percentage. Gaps over seven minutes are unknown; they are not counted as healthy. The rolling ledger is bounded in existing JSON details. Old incident history remains available; no historical 30-day coverage is fabricated.

## Operational limits

Vision localization can be uncertain; uncertainty takes the safe visual fallback and logs its reason. Sampling does not guarantee detection of every transient or every in-between-frame movement. Monitoring cannot durably write into an unreachable database; persistence failures are logged and missing observations remain unknown where observable. No short outage is promoted to a confirmed global Supabase incident. Existing ready rows with old wait metadata are not backfilled, and previously delivered films keep their existing appearance.
