# Spreelo v144.267 — Social connection succeeds even when free trial is already consumed

## Problem
A social account that had already consumed Spreelo's one-time free trial was rejected during trial preflight before the social connection was saved. This made an anti-abuse rule for the 100 free credits behave like a connection ban.

## Fix
- Trial-only restrictions are now non-blocking for social connection setup.
- Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads and Pinterest can still be connected when the free trial is unavailable.
- Trial authorization still refuses to award another free trial / 100 free credits.
- The successful OAuth result carries a `trial_notice` code back to Social Channels.
- Spreelo shows a clear message that the channel is connected but the 100 free credits were not added because the trial was already used by the social account, Spreelo account, or business.
- Paid-plan, admin-bypass, normal eligible-trial, plan-limit and unexpected-error behavior remains unchanged.

## Database
No new SQL migration is required for v144.267.
