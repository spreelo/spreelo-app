# Spreelo v144.234 — Shopify first-time onboarding routing

## What changed

- New Shopify App Store customers no longer land directly in Grow Brain after Shopify setup.
- If the Shopify install creates the user's first Spreelo brand workspace, Spreelo now continues to **Social channels** after Shopify connection/consent, matching the normal first-time Spreelo onboarding flow.
- Existing Spreelo customers still return to **Grow Brain** after adding Shopify; they are not forced through social-channel onboarding again.
- The decision is based on whether the account already had a Spreelo brand before the Shopify claim, not merely whether a new Shopify brand was created. This avoids treating an existing multi-brand customer as a brand-new Spreelo user.
- If the merchant selects **Not now** for Grow Brain's Shopify-data AI consent, a first-time customer still continues to Social channels instead of being dropped into Grow Brain.
- Brand analysis continues in the background after consent before routing onward.

## No database change

v144.234 does not require new SQL. It uses the v144.233 schema already deployed.

## Next planned step
