# Spreelo v144.273 — Growth Agent V4 Experiment Engine

- Adds a conservative experiment registry on top of the V3 Growth Profile.
- Experiments are deterministic, limited to safe content-format exploration/validation and capped to a 20% rolling-plan intent.
- No extra AI call is added. The existing planning call receives experiment context.
- Shadow mode records candidate behavior without changing the real plan.
- Active mode may softly nudge new plans; existing active calendar items are never rewritten.
- Experiment evidence is rechecked on later planning runs. After at least four new observations an active experiment can resolve as supported, not_supported or inconclusive.
- Existing rescue, Shopify, analysis, content generation and publishing engines are untouched.
