# Spreelo v144.167 — Market-Aware Protected Research Cost Guard

## Scope
This patch builds on v144.166 and only changes market-aware product research / protected-site discovery cost control.
No database migration or SQL is required.

## Changes
- The market-resolved storefront URL is now passed all the way into domain web research. Paid research no longer silently re-reads the older Brand-profile source after a target market has already been resolved.
- GPT product research receives an explicit target-market contract: same-market language variants are allowed, conflicting market assortments are not.
- Wrong-market-only research is bounded. One cheap correction pass is allowed; a third broad paid research round with the same market failure is not.
- Domain protection/rate-limit state is re-read immediately before product web research. If Store Map/local verification discovered a 403 after the caller's initial snapshot, research automatically switches to the inexpensive discovery model and enables the existing bounded exact-product repair path.
- The single-product fallback also refreshes protection state before choosing its paid research model, keeping downstream protected-authoritative selection consistent.
- Store Map shelf ranking filters obvious account/support/navigation artifacts (for example Customer Service, Club Boozt and Download) before AI shelf selection, so a tiny partial map on a protected retailer does not waste time crawling non-commerce shelves.
- Diagnostic logs now expose market-mismatch-only attempts, market-correction stopping, adaptive protected-site mode and filtered Store Map navigation nodes.

## Preserved behavior
- v144.166 category traversal/diversity and deterministic reserve-image variant guard remain unchanged.
- v144.165 GPT-5.6 Sol locked/proofread image-headline flow remains unchanged.
- Genuine calendar campaigns remain campaign-first and continue using explicit `queue_source = "campaign"` scope.
- Paid web research remains available when local/cheap protected-site discovery cannot produce a safe verified product.
- Social Channels is unchanged.
